# Theory

Music theory you can turn in your hands. Make a scale on a wheel and it tells you what you made.

**Use it:** https://mgilvryallen.github.io/theory/

- **The wheel:** two rings. Outside, all twelve notes. Inside, your scale, each note numbered from the **1**. Tap a spot to add a note or take it out, drag anywhere on the ring to carry the whole scale to a new key, and hold a note to make it the 1: same notes, new mode.
- **Or as drums:** switch to **drums** and the same two wheels stand on their side, rolling up and down with the 1 in the window, all twelve faces in view as they turn away.
- **Pick from the list:** tap the key or the scale for the whole list and the wheel whizzes round to it; the arrows step through.
- **It names what you make:** fifty-odd scales from the seven modes through harmonic and melodic minor and their modes, Phrygian dominant, double harmonic, Hungarian minor, the North Indian thaats, the pentatonics (Chinese and Japanese ones too), blues, whole tone, diminished and bebop. It says which mode of which parent it is, what other scales hide in the same notes, and, for one it can't name, which named scales are a single note away.
- **Hear it:** play the scale (each note pulses as it sounds; tap again to stop), tap any note, put a drone under it, or open [Drone](https://github.com/mgilvryallen/drone) in the same key.
- **The modes, side by side:** a grid from Lydian down to Locrian, each lowering one note of the one above. Compare each to the mode above it (the one note that changes), to major, or to minor: Dorian is minor with a raised 6, Mixolydian major with a ♭7.
- **Chords in the scale:** triads or sevenths on every degree, with Roman numerals. Tap one to hear it and see its notes light up on a number line that runs from the 1 to the next 1: change key and the numbers stay while the letters slide under them. Key and scale can be changed right there.
- **The circle of fifths:** fifths clockwise, fourths counterclockwise, key signatures, relative minors, the neighbors of whatever key is on the wheel, and a C-to-B number line, so stepping round you watch one note slide over each time. Its key arrows go round the circle.
- **Ear:** two notes, name the interval, with a tune to remember each one by. Between pairs, tap any interval to hear it, starting anywhere or from the 1 of your key.

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app**.

It works offline once it's opened once.

## Make your own copy

Click **Use this template** on GitHub, then **Settings → Pages**, source `main`. Everything is in `index.html`; the scales it knows are the `SCALES` list near the top of the script, one line each, so adding one is adding a line.
