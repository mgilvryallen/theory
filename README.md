# Theory

Music theory you can turn in your hands. Make a scale on a wheel and it tells you what you made.

**Use it:** https://mgilvryallen.github.io/theory/

- **The wheel:** three rings. Outside, all twelve notes. In the middle, the notes in your scale, lit: tap one to add it or take it out, drag the ring to carry the whole scale to a new key. Inside, the scale's degrees counted from the **1**: drag it (or use the arrows) to move the 1 and keep every note where it is, and you're in a new mode.
- **It names what you make:** fifty-odd scales from the seven modes through harmonic and melodic minor and their modes, Phrygian dominant, double harmonic, Hungarian minor, the North Indian thaats, the pentatonics (Chinese and Japanese ones too), blues, whole tone, diminished and bebop. It says which mode of which parent it is, what other scales hide in the same notes, and, for one it can't name, which named scales are a single note away.
- **Two roads to minor:** lower the 3, 6 and 7 (the parallel minor), or slide the 1 down to the 6 (the relative minor). Each one plays out on the wheel.
- **The modes, side by side:** a grid from Lydian down to Locrian, each lowering one note from the one above, marked against major, minor, or whichever is closer: Dorian is minor with a raised 6, Mixolydian major with a lowered 7.
- **Chords in the scale:** triads or sevenths on every degree, with Roman numerals. Tap to hear.
- **The circle of fifths:** key signatures, relative minors, and the neighbors of whatever key is on the wheel.
- **Ear:** two notes, name the interval, with a tune to remember each one by.
- **Hear it:** play the scale, any note, a drone under it, or open [Drone](https://github.com/mgilvryallen/drone) in the same key.

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app**.

It works offline once it's opened once.

## Make your own copy

Click **Use this template** on GitHub, then **Settings → Pages**, source `main`. Everything is in `index.html`; the scales it knows are the `SCALES` list near the top of the script, one line each, so adding one is adding a line.
