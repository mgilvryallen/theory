# Theory

Music theory you can turn in your hands. Make a scale on a wheel and it tells you what you made.

**Use it:** https://mgilvryallen.github.io/theory/

- **The wheel:** the outside ring is the twelve notes; the inside ring is your scale, each note numbered from the 1. Tap a dot to add a note or take one out, and drag the ring to move your scale to a new key.
- **The compass ring:** a thin ring just inside your notes shows where the 1 is. Drag its gold needle round (the small ticks turn with it) to move the 1 to another dot: same notes, new mode. F Ukrainian Dorian, needle to C: C harmonic minor.
- **The middle** plays your scale once; tap again to stop.
- **Handpan and drone:** under the wheel. The handpan improvises on your mode, like a handpan tuned to it, with an echo behind; the drone holds the 1 and 5. The scale plays over either. While one is playing and you scroll away, a small bar follows you down so you can switch them off (and on again).
- **Pick from the list:** tap the key or the scale for the whole list. A new key whizzes the wheel round; a scale with as many notes slides just the dots that change, and one with a different number of notes spins the wheel to it. The modes of major are listed brightest to darkest, so stepping through them one dot slides down a half step each time.
- **It names what you make:** fifty-odd scales from the seven modes through harmonic and melodic minor and their modes, Phrygian dominant, double harmonic, Hungarian minor, the North Indian thaats, the pentatonics (Chinese and Japanese ones too), blues, whole tone, diminished and bebop. It says which mode of which parent it is, what other scales hide in the same notes, and, for one it can't name, which named scales are a single note away.
- **Chords in the scale:** triads or sevenths on every degree, with Roman numerals. Tap one to hear it and see its notes light up on a number line that runs from the 1 to the next 1: change key and the numbers stay while the letters slide under them.
- **The circle of fifths:** fifths clockwise, fourths counterclockwise, key signatures, the neighbors of whatever key is on the wheel, and a C-to-B number line, so stepping round you watch one note slide over each time. Choose which modes the two rings show (major and minor to start; any two modes of major), and step the key by 4ths or 5ths.
- **Ear:** **test me** plays two notes for you to name, with a tune to remember each by; **explore** lets you tap any interval to hear it. Start anywhere or from the 1 of your key.

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app**.

It works offline once it's opened once.

## Make your own copy

Click **Use this template** on GitHub, then **Settings → Pages**, source `main`. Everything is in `index.html`; the scales it knows are the `SCALES` list near the top of the script, one line each, so adding one is adding a line.
